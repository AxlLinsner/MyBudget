MyBudget v4.12.71 — Full-Year Planned Bills

Changes from v4.12.70:
- Annual Dashboard Planned Bills now represents the full selected calendar year, including all scheduled occurrences from January through December.
- For the current year, the planned-bills chart continues to include currently active bill schedules only; inactive bill schedules and their records remain preserved.
- Recorded occurrence amounts are used, so amount changes during the year retain their period-specific planned amounts.
- Planned Bills labels now show the selected year (for example, Planned bills (2026)).
- Actual Bills Paid remains based on actual recorded bill transactions through today for the current year, and the full year for prior selected years.
- Monthly YTD calculations elsewhere in the Annual Dashboard are unchanged.

Preservation:
- No bill schedules, occurrences, transactions, payment records, or historical amounts are deleted or rewritten.
- No unrelated modules or calculations are intentionally changed.

Validation:
- JavaScript syntax check, HTML parsing/duplicate-ID check, version consistency, and ZIP integrity check.
- Browser interaction testing remains necessary.
