Forecasts & Reports

Forecast is a read-only view of your existing financial data. It uses your most recent complete spending history plus known future activity.
How Forecast works: Ordinary spending uses the 3 most recent complete months of actual expense history. The current incomplete month is not added to that historical baseline. The current month shows full Projected Spending and Remaining Spending separately. Future months continue using the same baseline until the current month is complete. Explicitly scheduled future income, bills, savings, and investments are shown separately from ordinary projected spending.
